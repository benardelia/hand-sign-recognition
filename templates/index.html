Recording Guidelines

  • Ensure your entire face, shoulders, and both hands are clearly visible in the camera frame.
  • Perform the gesture continuously throughout the recording period.
  • Try varying camera angles and hand positions slightly in each sequence to achieve a highly robust model.
  • Aim for at least **15-20 sequences per sign** for reliable accuracy.
